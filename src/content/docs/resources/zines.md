Short, self-published booklets meant to be read, printed, and passed on. The series below is hosted at [You Are Decolonized](https://youaredecolonized.vercel.app/), which documents how modern systems reproduce older forms of oppression under new branding — each issue follows the pattern "[X] is the new [Y]."

## You Are Decolonized series

- [Philanthropy Is the New Colonialism](https://youaredecolonized.vercel.app/zines/philanthropy/) — how charitable giving launders extraction and control.
- [Netflix Is the New Television](https://youaredecolonized.vercel.app/zines/netflix-television/) — streaming as the same attention economy, repackaged.
- [Uber Is the New Sharecropping](https://youaredecolonized.vercel.app/zines/uber-sharecropping/) — the gig economy and debt-bound labor.
- [Charter Schools Are the New Segregation](https://youaredecolonized.vercel.app/zines/charter-segregation/) — privatization and resegregation of public education.
- [Wellness Is the New Opium](https://youaredecolonized.vercel.app/zines/wellness-opium/) — individualized "self-care" as political sedation.
- [Social Media Is the New Panopticon](https://youaredecolonized.vercel.app/zines/social-media-panopticon/) — surveillance through participation.

Each issue can be read online and shared as social graphics. See [Graphics & Media](/resources/graphics/) for the shareable image sets.

:::tip[Print and share]
Zines are built to be reproduced. Read them online, then print and distribute — that is the point.
:::

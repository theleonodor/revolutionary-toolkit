External resources worth knowing. Every link here has been checked; if one breaks, flag it through the [Contributing Guide](/contributing/).

## Know your rights

- [ACLU — Know Your Rights](https://www.aclu.org/know-your-rights) — plain-language guides for protests, stops, and searches.
- [EFF — Know Your Rights](https://www.eff.org/issues/know-your-rights) — your rights over your devices and data.
- [National Lawyers Guild](https://www.nlg.org/) — legal observers, defense, and the protest support hotline.

## Digital security tools

- [Signal](https://signal.org/) — end-to-end encrypted messaging and calls.
- [Tor Project](https://www.torproject.org/) — anonymous browsing and circumvention.
- [Riseup](https://riseup.net/) — email, lists, and tools for organizers.
- [Security in a Box](https://securityinabox.org/en/) — practical digital-security guides for activists.

## Archives and texts

- [The Anarchist Library](https://theanarchistlibrary.org/) — full-text political and theoretical writing.
- [Marxists Internet Archive](https://www.marxists.org/) — primary-source library of historical writing.
- [Internet Archive](https://archive.org/) — books, documents, and historical media.

## Related projects

- [You Are Decolonized](https://youaredecolonized.vercel.app/) — zine series on how modern systems repackage old forms of control. See [Zines](/resources/zines/).
